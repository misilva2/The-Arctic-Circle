ABOUT US

The Arctic Circle has been a Bisbee tradition for generations. In the 1980s, Laverne Williams ran the original stand on Tombstone Canyon, and in the 1990s she handed it over to Terry Reyna. Terry kept the grill going until the late ’90s, when the doors closed and Bisbee went without its burger joint.

Terry’s younger brother, Bill Silva, had worked as a cook at the old Tombstone Canyon location. In February 2026, Terry and Bill brought The Arctic Circle back to life at its new home on SR-92, with the same family behind it and the same drive-in spirit.

Come see us Friday through Sunday for classic burgers, hotdogs, fries, and a cold drink. Limited seating available.
